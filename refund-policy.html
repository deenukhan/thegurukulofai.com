In short: everything we sell is a digital product, so all sales are final and we don't offer refunds. The only exceptions are payment errors and a session we cancel without a new date. Please check the date, time and requirements before you buy.

2. All sales are final

Our products are digital. Once your payment goes through, you get access to the session, files or content straight away, and that access can't be returned. For this reason:

  • We do not offer refunds, full or partial, once a purchase is complete.

Each product page lists what you need to take part, such as a Mac or Windows laptop, a stable internet connection, Zoom, and a paid plan for third-party software like Claude. These are not included in the price. Not having them, or a third-party tool changing its features, pricing or limits, is not a reason for a refund.

4. When we will refund you

We will refund you to your original payment method in these cases only:

  • You were charged twice for the same purchase. We refund the extra payment.
  • Money left your account but the payment failed and you got no access. We either give you access or refund you.
  • We cancel a live session and don't offer a new date. We refund the amount you paid for that session.

Approved refunds are usually processed within 5–7 working days. How long it then takes to reach your account depends on your bank or payment provider.

5. Rescheduling

Sometimes a live session has to move. If we reschedule, your seat moves to the new date and time automatically, and we tell you by email or WhatsApp. A rescheduled session is not cancelled, so it does not qualify for a refund.

For 1:1 sessions, you can move your booked call once if you tell us at least 24 hours before it starts. Calls missed without notice are counted as used.
