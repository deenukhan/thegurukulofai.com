Still have questions? Start here.

I've never edited a video. Is this for me?

Yes. You don't need Premiere Pro or any editing skills. You tell Claude what you want in plain English, and it does the cutting, captions and graphics. We start from zero and build the whole workflow together.

I don't know how to code. Can I still do this?

Yes. You talk to Claude in plain English and use copy-paste prompts. Claude writes and runs the code. If you can install an app on your laptop, you can follow along.

Won't AI edits look generic?

Only if you let it guess. You show Claude reels you like, approve a plan before anything is built, and every correction is saved. You can also make the cut yourself first and hand it to Claude for the rest.

Will it learn my style, or my client's?

Yes. You make one style folder per client or channel, so styles never mix. The masterclass shows you how to build one.

Do I have to stop using Premiere Pro, DaVinci or CapCut?

No. Claude takes the slow, repetitive parts, and you keep your editor for the parts you want to do by hand. Exporting a timeline back into Premiere or DaVinci is not covered in this session.
